# Z0C Allocation Policy

Status: local design reference. This document creates no allocation, balance,
contract, or token claim.

Z0C inherits the local Zeero L1 fair-launch envelope from
`ZEERO_L1_FIXED_SUPPLY_TOKENOMICS_V1`:

- Total monetary supply is capped at 50,000,000 ZEERO.
- No premine may create circulating supply before reviewed genesis and emission
  rules.
- No insider, venture, team, or reserve allocation is included in this model.
- New supply must be earned through reviewed network security work after an
  explicitly approved activation.

Z0C is a planned non-canonical Starknet representation for Z0Studio. It cannot
create a separate allocation, mint independently, or exceed the inherited
Zeero L1 supply envelope. Any future migration, redemption, or representation
contract remains blocked pending its own reviewed mechanism, target network,
compatibility evidence, and audit plan.
